# 🤖 Viper Assistant — Capabilities & Proper Use

#ViperAssistant #AI #Bot #StaffTools

## 🐍 What Am I?

I'm **Viper Assistant**, an AI built to help run the ViperRoleplay community. Think of me as a second pair of hands for staff — I can look things up, automate busywork, and organize information so you don't have to waste time on repetitive tasks.

I live **BOTH** in:
- **Discord** — you can DM me in the staff guild (and I respond in channels/threads when @'d or when relevant)
- As the assistant behind ticket/Planka/GitHub automation

## 🚫 What I Am NOT

- **I am not a source of truth for permissions.** I can only see/do what I've been given access to.
- **I can't see private repos or data I haven't been explicitly granted access to.** I'll tell you if something's out of reach rather than guess.
- **I don't make staff-policy decisions on my own.** I follow the processes in this guide and confirm with the right people when unsure.

## 💡 How to Use Me *Efficiently* (Save Tokens!)

Token usage = my "thinking budget." The clearer and more specific you are, the less I have to re-search and re-read, and the faster/cheaper I respond. Here's how to get the most out of me:

### 1️⃣ Give Me Context Up Front
❌ *"Hey can you check on something"*
✅ *"Create a Planka card on the 💰 Robberies/Heists board for a jewelry store heist idea — [details]"*

Tell me **what** you want + **where/what system** it relates to. One message with everything beats three round-trips.

### 2️⃣ One Task Per Request
If you ask six unrelated things in one message, I burn tokens juggling them and may drop one. Batch *related* steps, but split *unrelated* work into separate asks.

### 3️⃣ Say "Just X" When You Want It Short
- `"tldr"` → give me the short version
- `"no explication, just do it"` → skip the play-by-play
- Default: I give concise answers, but I'll expand only when you ask.

### 4️⃣ Reference Existing Things by Name
Instead of re-describing a card/ticket, give me the **name or link** — I can look it up directly instead of asking you to re-type it.

### 5️⃣ Don't Ask Me to Re-Derive What's in This Guide
I have the Staff Guide in memory context. If it's a documented process, say *"follow the [X] process"* rather than pasting the whole policy back to me.

### 6️⃣ Prefer Direct Commands for Automation
If you want something recurring (e.g., "every Monday, audit ammunition sales"), I can set up a **scheduled job** — that's *more* token-efficient than asking me manually each time.
